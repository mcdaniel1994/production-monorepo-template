## Context

The situation that forces a decision. What is true today, what pressure exists, and what constraints
are non-negotiable. Write this so it still makes sense to someone reading it in two years with no
memory of the conversation.

State the forces honestly — including the ones that pull against the decision below. If the context
section only supports one answer, it is probably incomplete.

## Decision

What we are doing, stated plainly and in the active voice. One decision per record.

Be specific enough to be checkable. "Use a relational database" is not a decision anyone can verify
compliance with; "Postgres 16, one database per service, migrations applied on deploy" is.

## Consequences

What becomes true as a result — the good and the bad.

- **Enables:** what this makes possible or easier.
- **Costs:** what this makes harder, slower, or more expensive. Every real decision has some.
- **Risks:** what could go wrong, and what would tell us early.
- **Follow-on work:** what now has to happen because of this.

An ADR with no costs listed has not been thought through.
